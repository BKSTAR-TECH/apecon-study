2.3 Price elasticity of demand

Everything so far has said which way quantity moves. Elasticity says how far — and that number decides most of the rest of the unit, from who bears a tax to what a price cut does to a firm’s takings.

Why the number is negative, and why nobody says so

Why the number comes out negative

The law of demand guarantees that price and quantity demanded move in opposite directions, so the raw calculation always comes out negative. The exam compares the magnitude — the absolute value — against 1, and an answer choice reading “−2.5” and one reading “2.5” are describing the same demand. Getting a negative number is the law of demand confirming itself, not an error to hunt for.

When the price goes up, the amount people buy goes down. That is the law of demand. So one percent change is positive and the other is negative, and dividing them always gives a negative answer. Everybody ignores this. The exam compares the size of the number to 1 and does not care about the sign. An answer choice that reads −2.5 and one that reads 2.5 are describing the same demand. If your answer comes out negative, nothing has gone wrong. That is the law of demand agreeing with you.

Worked example — the arithmetic, twice A store raises a price from $200 to $220 and quantity demanded falls by 20 per cent.
The percentage price change: $20 on $200 is 10 per cent.
The elasticity: 20 divided by 10 is 2. Above 1, so demand is elastic over that range.
And the revenue check: price rose, demand is elastic, so total revenue fell. Quantity gave up a fifth to buy a tenth on price, which is a bad trade.
Worked example — four stepsA shop raises a price from $200 to $220. The amount people buy falls by 20%.
Step 1. The price change, in percent. The price went up by $20. $20 out of $200 is 10%.
Step 2. Divide. 20% divided by 10% is 2.
Step 3. Compare it to 1. 2 is bigger than 1, so demand is elastic over this range.
Step 4. What happened to the money? The shop gained 10% on the price and lost 20% of its sales. It collects less money than before. That is a bad trade.
